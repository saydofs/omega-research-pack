# Contributing to Omega Interlevel Numbers

This project welcomes rigorous, constructive mathematical criticism.

## What is especially useful

Contributions are most valuable when they include one or more of:

- a precise counterexample;
- a corrected or simplified definition;
- a formal proof;
- a proof that a claimed property fails;
- an alternative geometric embedding;
- an alternative algebra that better preserves Omega-NR principles;
- reproducible computational experiments;
- comparison with prior mathematical literature;
- a concrete application demonstrating added value.

## Please separate four kinds of statements

When opening an issue, review, or proposal, label the content conceptually as:

1. **Axiom** — assumed starting principle.
2. **Definition** — introduced mathematical convention.
3. **Proposition/Theorem** — claimed consequence requiring proof.
4. **Experiment/Observation** — computational or empirical evidence.

Do not treat an experiment as a proof.

## Constructive criticism

Direct criticism is welcome. The project does not require agreement with Omega-NR.

However, when practical, criticism is especially helpful when accompanied by:

- a minimal failing example;
- the exact definition or equation affected;
- a proposed repair;
- or a stronger alternative construction.

The objective is to determine whether the framework can be refined, falsified, generalized, or replaced by something more rigorous.

## Attribution

If a contribution materially changes the mathematical framework, preserve a clear authorship and provenance record.

Contributors retain rights in their own contributions subject to the licensing terms they explicitly accept when submitting them.

## Substantive changes to the official framework

A proposed change is considered substantive when it alters, for example:

- the Omega Numerical Uniqueness Axiom;
- the definition of an Omega Interlevel Number;
- the role of zero or structural boundaries;
- the realization-identity rule;
- a canonical operation;
- the Local Emergence Law;
- the canonical geometry;
- a published theorem or proof;
- the interpretation of structural versus realization identity.

For such proposals, please provide:

1. the current statement;
2. the proposed replacement;
3. mathematical justification;
4. proof or counterexample where applicable;
5. reproducible computational evidence where applicable;
6. known consequences for earlier results.

Independent proposals are welcome, but they do not become part of the official framework until explicitly accepted into an official release.

## Commercial proposals

Commercial licensing, paid integrations, partnerships, or revenue-generating use of covered project materials should be handled separately from scientific contribution review and require an appropriate written agreement where the existing license does not grant the requested rights.
